# Teammates and roles

Open **Team** and choose **Invite teammate**. The invite is valid for 7 days.

Each plan has a seat limit (see [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md)). Pending invites count as seats. You cannot invite someone when every seat is used.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing and deleting the account. One per account. |
| Admin | Manage invoices, clients, settings and teammates. Cannot change billing. |
| Member | Create and edit invoices and clients. Cannot change settings or invite people. |
| Viewer | Read only. |

Change a role from the teammate's row. Removing a teammate frees their seat immediately.
