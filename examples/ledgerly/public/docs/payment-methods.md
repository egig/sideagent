# Payment methods

## Getting paid

Clients can pay by card or bank transfer. Connect your payout account under **Settings → Payouts**. Payouts arrive 2 business days after a payment.

## Your own card (for Ledgerly)

Your plan is charged to the card saved under **Billing → Payment method**.

If a charge fails, Ledgerly retries it after 3 days and again after 5 more days, and emails you each time. Common reasons:

- `card_declined`: the bank refused the charge. Contact your bank or use another card.
- `expired_card`: update the card under **Billing → Payment method**.
- `insufficient_funds`: add funds, then choose **Retry payment** on the billing page.

After the last retry fails, your account moves to the Free plan. Your data is kept. Paid features such as extra seats turn off until a payment succeeds.
