# Plans and billing

| Plan | Price | Invoices | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 3 per month | 1 |
| Pro | $19 per month | Unlimited | 5 |
| Business | $49 per month | Unlimited | 20 |

## Changing plans

Open **Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades apply at once and you pay the prorated difference. Downgrades apply at the end of the billing period. A downgrade is blocked while you have more seats in use than the new plan allows.

## Invoices from Ledgerly

Your own Ledgerly receipts are under **Billing → Receipts**. You are charged once per period. If you see two charges, one is usually a pending authorization that your bank drops within 5 business days. If both stay after that, contact support with the receipt numbers.

## Cancelling

Choose **Billing → Cancel plan**. You keep paid features until the period ends.
