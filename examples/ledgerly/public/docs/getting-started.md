# Getting started

Ledgerly lets you send invoices and get paid in minutes.

1. **Create your account.** Sign up with your work email and name your business.
2. **Add a client.** Open **Clients**, choose **New client**, and enter a name and email.
3. **Create an invoice.** Choose **New invoice**, pick the client, add line items, and select **Send**.
4. **Get paid.** Your client gets a link to pay by card or bank transfer. You see the status change to **Paid** as soon as the money arrives.

Every account starts on the **Free** plan: 3 invoices a month and 1 seat. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md) to upgrade.
