# Creating invoices

Open **Invoices**, then **New invoice**.

## Fields

- **Client**: who you bill. Their email receives the invoice.
- **Due date**: defaults to 14 days after the issue date. Change the default in **Settings**.
- **Line items**: description, quantity and unit price. Totals update as you type.
- **Notes**: shown at the bottom of the invoice.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Not sent yet. You can still edit it. |
| Sent | Delivered to the client, not paid. |
| Overdue | Past the due date and not paid. Ledgerly sends a reminder 3 days and 10 days after the due date. |
| Paid | Payment received. |

Sent invoices cannot be edited. Void the invoice and create a new one instead. A voided invoice number is never reused.
